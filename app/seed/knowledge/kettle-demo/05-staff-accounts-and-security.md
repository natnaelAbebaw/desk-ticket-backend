# Staff accounts and security

## Roles

- Owner: everything, including bank details and closing the account.
- Manager: menu, staff, refunds, and reports.
- Cashier: take orders and payments. Refunds need a manager's PIN.

## Logging in on the POS

Staff log in on the POS with a 4-digit PIN. PINs are per location. Five wrong PINs in a
row lock that staff member out for 15 minutes.

## Two-step verification

Owners and managers must turn on two-step verification for the dashboard. If an owner
loses their phone, support can only reset two-step verification after a video call that
checks their photo ID against the account. Agents must never reset it over email or
chat, even for a customer they know.

## Bank details

Only the owner can change the bank account for payouts. After a change, payouts pause
for 3 days and the old address gets an email, which gives the real owner time to react
if an attacker made the change.
