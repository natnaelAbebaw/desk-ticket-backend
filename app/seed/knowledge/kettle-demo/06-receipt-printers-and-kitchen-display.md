# Receipt printers and kitchen display

## Supported printers

Kettle works with 80 mm thermal printers over the network (Ethernet or Wi-Fi). USB
printers work only with the K-Station tablet stand.

## Printer not printing

1. Check the paper roll: thermal paper prints only on one side.
2. Print a self-test by holding the feed button while turning the printer on. The
   self-test shows the printer's IP address.
3. In the POS, open Settings, then Hardware, and check that the printer's IP address
   matches the self-test. Routers can hand out a new address after a power cut; give
   the printer a fixed (reserved) address on the router to stop this happening.

## Kitchen display (KDS)

The kitchen display shows each order as a ticket, color-coded by age: white under 5
minutes, amber from 5 to 10, red after 10. If orders stop appearing, check that the
display and the POS are on the same Wi-Fi network; guest networks often block devices
from seeing each other.
