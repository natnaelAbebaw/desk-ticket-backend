# Refunds and chargebacks

## Refunds

A sale can be refunded in full or in part for up to 120 days. Open the order in the
Kettle dashboard or the POS app, choose Refund, pick the items or enter an amount, and
confirm. The money returns to the same card in 5 to 10 business days. Kettle cannot
refund to a different card or in cash on the card's behalf; cash refunds for card sales
come out of the till and are the merchant's choice.

A refund is taken from the next payout. If the next payout is too small, the rest is
taken from the following payouts, and a refund is never blocked by a low balance.

## Chargebacks

A chargeback is when a cardholder asks their bank to reverse a payment. Kettle emails
the merchant the same day and holds the disputed amount plus a 15 USD fee. The merchant
has 7 days to send evidence through the dashboard: the receipt, a photo of the order,
and any messages with the customer. The fee is returned if the merchant wins.

Agents should tell merchants to respond even when the amount is small: losing many
chargebacks can lead to a payout hold.
