# Online ordering and menu sync

## How menus sync

The menu is edited once in the dashboard and published to the POS, the Kettle ordering
page, and connected delivery partners. Changes reach the POS within a minute and
delivery partners within 5 minutes.

## Sold-out items

Marking an item as sold out (86'd) in the POS hides it everywhere until the end of the
day, or until it is marked available again. Items come back automatically at the
location's opening time the next day.

## Modifiers

Modifiers are the options on an item, such as milk type or extra shot. A modifier group
can be required or optional and can have a price. Delivery partners show at most 10
options per group; options past the tenth are dropped on their side.

## Sync errors

If a delivery partner rejects the menu, the dashboard shows a red banner with the
partner's reason. The common causes are an item with no price, a photo larger than
5 MB, or a name longer than 60 characters. Fix the item and publish again. Orders
already placed are not affected by a failed sync.
