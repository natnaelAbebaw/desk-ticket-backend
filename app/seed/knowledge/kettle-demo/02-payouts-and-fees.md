# Payouts and fees

## Payout schedule

Card sales are paid out to the merchant's bank account every business day. Money from
a sale arrives 2 business days later: a Friday sale lands on Tuesday. Instant payouts,
which arrive within 30 minutes, cost 1 percent of the amount and are available after a
location has processed payments for 30 days.

## Fees

In-person card payments cost 2.6 percent plus 10 cents. Online orders cost 2.9 percent
plus 30 cents. Refunds return the percentage fee but not the fixed part. There is no
monthly fee for the first location; each extra location is 29 USD a month.

## Tips

Card tips are paid out with the sales they belong to, in the same payout. Cash tips are
recorded for reporting only and never move money. Tip pooling is set up in Settings,
Staff, Tips; changes apply to shifts that start after the change.

## Payout holds

A payout can be held when Kettle's risk team sees unusual activity: a sudden jump in
volume, many refunds, or several chargebacks in a week. The merchant gets an email with
the reason and the documents needed, usually a recent bank statement and supplier
invoices. Most holds are reviewed within 3 business days of receiving the documents.
Support agents cannot release a hold themselves; they add a note to the ticket and
assign it to the risk team.
